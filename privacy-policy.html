Privacy Policy for City Favorite Foods

Effective date: October 20, 2026

City Favorite Foods is a local-food discovery app. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Information collection and use

City Favorite Foods does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information. The app does not require an account, analytics, advertising identifiers, location permission, contacts, camera, microphone, or device storage access. City posters and dish artwork are bundled with the app and do not require access to your photos or files.

External links

When you choose directions for a dish, the app opens a map application or website. That service is governed by its own privacy policy. City Favorite Foods does not receive your map searches or location data.

Images and licenses

The app includes food photographs from Wikimedia Commons and city-card artwork and cartoon dish illustrations supplied for this app. Wikimedia author names, source links, and licenses are listed in the image credits. No external author, source URL, or license details were included with the supplied artwork files; the credits page identifies those assets by city pack without claiming authorship or a particular license.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app's data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when changes are made.
